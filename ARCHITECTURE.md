# NEXUS Blockchain Architecture

NEXUS Blockchain is an independent system. NEXUS Messenger messages are not stored on-chain and do not require blockchain hashes or transactions. NEXUS AI remains separate.

## V0.1 roadmap
1. Core block and chain model
2. Signed transactions
3. Wallet and key management
4. Persistent storage
5. P2P networking
6. Consensus
7. Multi-node testnet
8. RPC/API
9. Asset economics
10. Mainnet hardening
